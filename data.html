Data

The numbers behind every page, taken from each person's 12 Axes full report. Reference figures come from the site's public personality pages. Homestead and the Other Homestead were computed here, then run through the site.

  • profiles.csv

    One row per person: top ideology, family, match %, closest country, historical match, figure, parties, era, compass label, other and furthest ideologies, top book. Includes Homestead and the Other Homestead.

  • axes_wide.csv

    Everyone’s 12 axis positions in one table (0 = first pole, 100 = second pole, 50 = Balanced), plus the two compass coordinates used on page 1. Includes the four reference figures.

  • axes_long.csv

    The same positions, one row per person per axis, with the report’s own label (for example “89% Autocracy”) and the value the 12 Axes site expects in a share link.

  • figures.csv

    Each report’s eight closest figures with match %, and its three furthest figures.

  • places_top10.csv

    Each report’s “10 countries or regions to live in” with match %.

  • cabinets.csv

    Every ideal-government cabinet, seat by seat. For Homestead and the Other Homestead it also lists the friend-based guess and whose cabinet it came from.

  • homestead_derivation.csv

    How Homestead and the Other Homestead were built: each axis’s top-3 trio on both sides, their averages, and which side each version took.

  • data.json

    Everything above in one JSON file, plus axis definitions and the 12 Axes share links.

Axes the reports mark “Balanced” have no number, so they are stored as 50. Homestead and the Other Homestead are built from 11 of the 12 members. 12 Axes notes that its matches are not a scientific diagnosis.
