Homestead Ideal Government

Averaging the group washes everything toward the middle. Homestead does the opposite: on every axis, the loudest side wins.

01 Top 3 on each side For each axis, take the three people furthest toward one pole and the three furthest toward the other.
02 Average each trio Each side gets one number: the average strength of its three strongest members.
03 Greater value wins The side with the higher average sets Homestead's position. Then each cabinet seat goes to whichever friend's pick fits that position best.

The cabinet

The site's own cabinet. The tag shows whether the friend-based guess from before got the same person.
Political leader Guess: Charlemagne (vald)
Leonid Brezhnev Weighs Representation (86 Autocracy) and Economy (85 Public). Runs the government as a whole, sets the central priorities and answers for the major decisions that affect the entire country. Politically, would concentrate decisions in a small, stable core, with no strict term limits, picking cadres by competence or succession instead of relying on the vote. On the economy, would steer the government with strategic companies and public banks at the core of development, curbing the power of large private groups and expanding land reform.
Deputy political leader Guess: Józef Piłsudski (angry)
Xi Jinping Weighs Structure (81 Unitary) and Diplomacy (80 Militarist). Supports the leader day to day and represents the country abroad, handling alliances, negotiations and relations with other governments. On structure, would make sure states and cities follow the central government’s line, speaking with one voice abroad and keeping regions from pursuing parallel deals or policies. On diplomacy, would treat military strength as the foundation of foreign policy, with strong armed forces, a domestic defense industry and credible deterrence against any threat.
Head of Economy Guess matched
Friedrich List Weighs Economy (85 Public) and Control (80 Planning). Manages public finances, taxes and the direction of the economy, deciding how the country produces, spends and distributes income. On the economy, would prioritize strong public services and state-owned companies, with public banks financing development, a basic income for those in need and wealth redistribution. On control, would plan strategic sectors, use subsidies, exchange rates and public spending to steer the economy and keep the central bank aligned with the elected government.
Head of Education Guess: Max Weber (angry)
Sergei Korolev Weighs Morality (91 Traditionalist) and Religion (96 Irreligious). Defines what schools and universities teach and how they shape new generations, from the curriculum to access to higher education. On morals, would leave moral and sex education to families, value the traditional family model and prefer gradual changes to the curriculum. On religion, would keep education secular, with no religious symbols in schools, no religion in early childhood education and values taught without relying on belief in God.
Chief of Political Strategy Guess: Ferdinand Lassalle (angry)
Ramiro Ledesma Ramos Weighs Diplomacy (80 Militarist) and Intervention (90 Nationalist). Builds majorities, designs the rules of the political game and plans the country’s place in the world over the medium and long term. On diplomacy, would frame the country’s position around military power, with a domestic defense industry, priority for arms spending and deterrence as the basis of any negotiation. On intervention, would treat regional influence as a strategic goal, using alliances, sanctions and leadership among neighbors to increase the country’s weight.
Philosophical Adviser Guess: Joseph de Maistre (vald)
Oswald Spengler Weighs Power (73 Security) and Morality (91 Traditionalist). Helps the government connect principles to concrete decisions, pointing out which values are at stake in each hard choice. On power, would remind everyone that freedom depends on protection, arguing that the community should accept certain restrictions to contain crime, disinformation and serious threats. On morals, would treat family, marriage and inherited customs as the foundations of shared life, preferring slow change and distrusting moral ruptures.
Coordinator of Social Affairs Guess: Raphael Machado (vald)
Che Guevara Weighs Immigration (88 Assimilation) and Representation (86 Autocracy). Coordinates the policies that touch people’s everyday lives, such as family, social protection, inclusion and coexistence between groups. On immigration, would make integration a priority, with mandatory language courses and an expectation of adapting to local customs, avoiding culturally isolated neighborhoods. Politically, would decide social programs from a lean technical core, prioritizing quick results and stability over popular consultations and lengthy debates.
Coordinator of Industry and Innovation Guess: Alex Karp (angry)
Rong Yiren Weighs Technology (82 Technology) and Trade (69 Protectionism). Oversees production, research and innovation, deciding how the country develops its industry, its energy and new technologies. On technology, would speed up automation, artificial intelligence, biotechnology and space exploration, accepting environmental impacts in exchange for industrial growth. On trade, would protect domestic industry with tariffs and public procurement and block the sale of strategic companies to foreigners.
