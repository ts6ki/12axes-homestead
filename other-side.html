What if we took the other side?

Same method as Homestead, same 12 people, one change: on every axis, the side that lost takes the seat instead. Call it the Other Homestead.

01 Top 3 on each side For each axis, take the three people furthest toward one pole and the three furthest toward the other.
02 Average each trio Each side gets one number: the average strength of its three strongest members.
03 Smaller value wins The side with the lower average sets the position this time. Cabinet seats are filled exactly as on page 3.

The cabinet

The site's own cabinet. The tag shows whether the friend-based guess from before got the same person.
Political leader Guess: James Madison (waqst)
Jimmy Carter Weighs Representation (83 Democracy) and Economy (69 Private). Runs the government as a whole, sets the central priorities and answers for the major decisions that affect the entire country. Politically, would govern under the check of free elections, independent courts and an active opposition, making room for referendums and the recall of elected officials. On the economy, would shrink the public sector’s role, opening services, roads and state-owned companies to private enterprise and shielding property and inheritance from redistribution.
Deputy political leader Guess: Otto von Habsburg (waqst)
Charles III Weighs Structure (64 Federal) and Diplomacy (55 Pacifist, inside the balanced range). Supports the leader day to day and represents the country abroad, handling alliances, negotiations and relations with other governments. On structure, would bridge the central and local governments, defending the autonomy of states and cities and making room for them to strike their own agreements abroad. On diplomacy, would combine a well-prepared defense with active diplomacy, negotiating whenever possible but never giving up the military capacity needed to deter attacks.
Head of Economy Guess: Roberto Campos (tsuki)
Friedrich Hayek Weighs Economy (69 Private) and Control (62 Free market). Manages public finances, taxes and the direction of the economy, deciding how the country produces, spends and distributes income. On the economy, would hand the management of services and infrastructure to the private sector, protect property and inheritance and set a maximum duration for welfare benefits. On control, would leave prices, wages and interest rates to the market, cut taxes and regulations, defend an independent central bank and avoid price freezes.
Head of Education Guess: Alexandre de Moraes (dumfries)
Bob Dylan Weighs Morality (76 Progressive) and Religion (78 Religious). Defines what schools and universities teach and how they shape new generations, from the curriculum to access to higher education. On morals, would include sex education and education on gender identity, adopt racial quotas for university admission and treat family diversity as school content. On religion, would make room for faith in students’ upbringing, valuing the passing on of religious traditions and the role of faith communities in education.
Chief of Political Strategy Guess: Otto Strasser (vald)
Carlo Rosselli Weighs Diplomacy (55 Pacifist) and Intervention (54 Non-interventionist), both inside the balanced range. Builds majorities, designs the rules of the political game and plans the country’s place in the world over the medium and long term. On diplomacy, would build a strategy that combines defense capacity and diplomacy, keeping force as a deterrent and negotiation as the preferred path. On intervention, would seek a pragmatic stance, forming alliances of convenience and defending the country’s own interests without being tied to blocs or long interventions.
Philosophical Adviser Guess: Leonard Peikoff (tsuki)
John Locke Weighs Power (71 Liberty) and Morality (76 Progressive). Helps the government connect principles to concrete decisions, pointing out which values are at stake in each hard choice. On power, would argue that every adult owns their own life, body and private choices, and that surveillance and punishment require very strong justification. On morals, would defend equal rights for LGBT people, reproductive autonomy and respect for new family models, seeing morality as something that evolves.
Coordinator of Social Affairs Guess matched
Al Gore Weighs Immigration (54 Multiculturalism, inside the balanced range) and Representation (83 Democracy). Coordinates the policies that touch people’s everyday lives, such as family, social protection, inclusion and coexistence between groups. On immigration, would encourage learning the local language and coexistence between groups, without forcing anyone to give up their own traditions. Politically, would shape social policy through direct public consultation, assemblies and community councils, giving minorities a voice and leaving room for the opposition’s criticism.
Coordinator of Industry and Innovation Guess matched
John Mackey Weighs Technology (68 Biology) and Trade (64 Globalism). Oversees production, research and innovation, deciding how the country develops its industry, its energy and new technologies. On technology, would give priority to environmental preservation, fewer pesticides and lower-impact production, even at the cost of lost productivity or slower growth. On trade, would integrate industry into global supply chains, attracting multinationals and foreign investment.
